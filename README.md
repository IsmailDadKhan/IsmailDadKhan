## 👋 About Me

I started out managing e-commerce brands and social media campaigns. Somewhere along the way I realised the numbers behind those campaigns were more interesting to me than the posts themselves, and that pulled me into data, code, and AI.

I just finished my **BS in Computer Science (Data Science domain)** at **Hamdard University, Karachi** (May 2026). Coursework covered AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Warehousing & Mining, and Data Visualization. Since then I've been building projects that try to solve real problems, not just tutorial exercises.

📍 Karachi, Pakistan
🔍 **Open to:** Data Analyst, Data Scientist, BI / Power BI, and AI-related roles (internships and entry-level)

## 🚀 Featured Projects

| Project | What it does | Built with |
|---|---|---|
| **BakhabarAI** 🚨<br/>*Google AI Seekho 2026 Hackathon (team project)* | A multi-agent system that spots urban emergencies in Pakistani cities by combining social media, weather, traffic, and citizen reports. It can reason about cascading events (like flooding cutting off routes to cooling centres during a heatwave) and has false-alarm detection with automatic alert retraction. **12x faster response** than a rule-based baseline. | Google ADK (Antigravity), Flutter, Firebase, Google Maps / Weather / Places APIs |
| **Musafir** 🚌<br/>*National AI Hackathon 2026, Karachi (team project)* | An AI journey planner for Karachi's public transit (BRT, buses, Chinchis). Tell it where you want to go by text or voice and it plans the full trip. **Top 5 out of 60+ teams.** | Python, NLP, real local transit data |
| **Dakhla** 🎓<br/>*Final Year Project (in progress)* | An AI-assisted university admissions platform with personalised recommendations, a chatbot (DakhlaBot), and one-click applications to multiple universities. | AI / ML, chatbot, web platform |
| **Sales Analysis & Customer Insights** 📊<br/>*Data Warehousing & Mining* | End-to-end pipeline: Python ETL, Star Schema design, customer segmentation with clustering, and visualisations of sales trends and payment methods. | Python, SQL, Pandas, Matplotlib |
| **Spam SMS Detector** 📩 | Text classifier that separates spam from genuine messages using NLP (cleaning, tokenisation, TF-IDF). | Python, NLP, Naive Bayes / Logistic Regression |
